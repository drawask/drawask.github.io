DWG to ChatGPT (copy drawing context for AI)

ChatGPT cannot read a raw DWG well by itself. CadChat turns the drawing into a clean text pack (tables, layers, scoped entities) you can paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Cursor.

Open DWG and copy for ChatGPT

Best prompts after you paste

  • “Summarize pipe sizes and stations from the tables.”
  • “List ground elevation vs invert at each station.”
  • “Estimate excavation depth ranges from the datasheet.”
  • “What is missing for a quantity takeoff?”

Why this works better than uploading DWG to chat

  • Structured tables beat opaque binary CAD files
  • You control scope (layers/region) before the AI sees it
  • File stays local until you paste the text pack yourself
