How to convert DWG to CSV

  1. Open drawask.github.io
  2. Drop a .dwg or .dxf file
  3. Explore layers in the playground if you want a scoped region
  4. Go to Ask AI and download CSV

What kinds of tables work best?

  • Civil plan/profile elevation grids (GROUND, TOP PIPE, DEPTH, STATION)
  • Drawing index sheets (PART, FROM ST, DRAWING NO.)
  • Labeled numeric schedules on content layers

Why use CadChat for DWG to CSV?

  • Free online DWG viewer + table extract
  • Local parsing (privacy-friendly)
  • Also builds a chat pack for ChatGPT / Claude
